# Privacy Policy — WIMS (Where Is My Stat)

Last updated: 10 September 2026

## The short version

WIMS doesn't collect anything. There's no account, no analytics, no
advertising and no tracking. Your scorebook lives on your phone, and — if you
leave iCloud sync on — in your own iCloud account. It is never sent to us,
because there is no "us" to send it to: WIMS has no server.

## What WIMS stores

Everything you enter while using the app:

- Teams, player names and jersey numbers
- Games, lineups, and the full play-by-play of every game you score
- Seasons, league rules and app settings

This is stored on your device. We have no access to it.

## iCloud

If iCloud sync is switched on (Settings → Storage), the same data is mirrored
to **your** iCloud account using Apple's CloudKit private database. That storage
belongs to you and counts against your iCloud quota. Apple's privacy policy
governs it. The developer of WIMS cannot read, retrieve or delete it.

You can turn sync off at any time in Settings → Storage.

## Player names you enter

WIMS lets you record the names of other people — your teammates, opponents and
substitutes. That information is yours, held on your device and in your iCloud,
and is never transmitted to the developer. If you record names of other people,
including anyone under 18, you are responsible for doing so appropriately. WIMS
never shares that data with anyone.

## When data leaves your device

Only when you choose to send it. WIMS can export a game, a season or your whole
scorebook as a PDF, a CSV or a JSON file, and can share a single game with
another scorekeeper. Those files go wherever you send them — a message, an
email, iCloud Drive — and that transfer is entirely under your control. The
developer receives no copy.

## What WIMS does not do

- No account or sign-up
- No analytics or crash-reporting services
- No advertising, and no advertising identifiers
- No third-party SDKs
- No tracking across apps or websites
- No selling or sharing of data, because none is collected

## Deleting your data

Deleting the app removes everything stored on the device. Data in your iCloud is
managed through your Apple ID — see Settings → [your name] → iCloud → Manage
Account Storage on your device. Export a backup first if you want to keep it.

## Changes

If a future version of WIMS collects anything, this policy will be updated
before that version ships, and the change will be described here rather than
buried.
